A Hundred Years of Service Above Self



Our Why

For 100 years, the Rotary Club of Tarpon Springs has been committed to strengthening our community through service. Our greatest legacy has been investing in the future of local students.

Through the Rotary Youth Trust and our partnership with the Pinellas Education Foundation's Take Stock in Children program, we've helped provide life-changing scholarships to at-risk students. A college or technical education doesn't just transform one student's future—it creates opportunities that can change an entire family for generations.

To honor the legacy of Rotarian Dan Cullu, whose vision helped establish our scholarship program, we are creating the Dan Cullu Memorial Scholarship Endowment. Our goal is to build a $100,000 permanent fund that will provide a scholarship every year, forever.

Join us in creating opportunities, honoring Dan's legacy, and changing lives—one student at a time.

A Century of Service. A Legacy That Lives On.

Questions

Frequently Asked



What should I wear? +

Black-tie optional. We warmly encourage roaring-20s glamour — think art deco elegance, feathers, sequins, and sharp tuxedos. Come dressed for a century worth celebrating.

Where exactly is the venue? +

The gala is held in the Stirling Hall, Innisbrook Resort, Palm Harbor, Florida. Complimentary valet parking is available on the evening of the event.

Can I reserve a full table? +

Yes. Full tables are reserved through our sponsorship tiers:

  • 1 Table (8 seats): Available with the Bronze ($1,500) tier, which includes company name recognition.
  • 2 Tables (16 seats): Available with Silver ($3,000), Gold ($5,000), Diamond ($7,500), or Presenting ($15,000) tiers, which come with added logo exposure and VIP perks.
Are dietary needs accommodated? +

Absolutely. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and allergy-conscious options are available — simply note your preferences when you reserve.

Will proceeds support the community? +

Yes. Proceeds support the Rotary Club of Tarpon Springs' service projects, scholarships, and community programs — the heart of our next hundred years.
